# Design Direction — IT Trattoria

> 350-word direction statement. Builds on `/research/00-synthesis.md`. The design language is locked here; everything else (tokens, components, photography) flows from this.

## In one paragraph

A disciplined cream-and-charcoal editorial chrome — Don Angie / Via Carota restraint — broken by load-bearing hand-drawn illustrations carried over from IT's existing equity (the Vespa rider, the couple toasting, the pasta-mountain, the family with *La Gazzetta dello Sport*), with the green monogram, peperoncino vermilion, bergamot mustard, and Ionian sea-green deployed sparingly as Calabrian accents. Editorial serif display (Instrument Serif in the mockup; PP Editorial New or GT Sectra in production) paired with a neutral sans (Geist). Italian dialect labels as quiet texture (Aperitivo · Al Banco · A Tavola · Dolce — never full translation). Photography under one discipline: overhead, natural light, a single plate on warm linen, with the food not posed but caught mid-eating. Motion is restrained — pace signals confidence by refusing to oversell. The site's spine is a regional-Italy interactive map; Calabria is the front door. That map is the single most distinctive design move and is what makes this site Awwwards-tier rather than another counter-service template.

## Tone keywords

- Honest, not nostalgic
- Editorial, not corporate
- Warm, not cute
- Confident, not loud
- Italian, not Italian-American
- Calabrian-rooted, not Tuscany-flavored

## Anti-patterns (do not ship)

- The tricolore flag as a primary brand element
- Generic Italian-trattoria red-checkered-tablecloth visual cues
- "Welcome to..." / "Discover..." / "Experience..." headlines
- Stock food photography of "rustic" wooden boards
- Centered, gradient-blob hero with three feature cards underneath
- Reservation theater (counter-service does not reserve)
- AI-generated food photos that look AI-generated
- Glassmorphism for the sake of it
- Purple-to-pink gradients of any kind

## Direct visual references

- **Don Angie** — register, editorial about page, candid founder portrait
- **Via Carota** — restraint, type discipline, photography rule
- **Tatte** — photographic consistency
- **CAVA** — location-first ordering, builder UX
- **Sweetgreen** — sourcing as UI element
- **L'Industrie Pizzeria** — confident counter-service vibe
- **Caffè Milani** (per R5) — magazine as nav item
- **Da Maria** (per R5) — editorial type-over-image hero with rotating "today's plate"

## What's load-bearing

- The **green IT monogram** (top-left of every page)
- The **hand-drawn illustrations** (carry the warmth that photography can't on stub pages)
- The **regional-Italy map** (Calabria-centric front door + nav spine)
- **Cream + charcoal** as ~90% of all chrome, with accent color used in single-instance moments
- **Italian section labels** as quiet typographic texture
- **Restraint** as the dominant design move
