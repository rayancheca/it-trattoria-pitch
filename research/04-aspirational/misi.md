# Misi — Tear-down

**URL:** https://www.misinewyork.com
**Priority:** High

---

## Type pairing
Serif wordmark ("Misi" in a refined, slightly modern serif) over clean sans-serif body and navigation. Less ornate than Carbone, less old-world than Via Carota — sits in a contemporary upscale-minimalist register. Hierarchy is built through scale and weight rather than ornament.

## Color system
Monochromatic and gallery-like:
- White / near-white backgrounds (`#FFFFFF` / `#FAFAFA`)
- Black or deep charcoal text (`#0E0E0E`-ish)
- Asset references like "50blk.png" suggest a strict black-and-white system with photography providing all color
- The Brooklyn waterfront / Domino Sugar Factory context bleeds in through warm food and brick imagery
Cleanest, most "design-forward" palette of the set.

## Motion style
Restrained. Hero photo carousel with quiet slide transitions. No scroll-driven storytelling, no parallax. The discipline of the motion matches the discipline of Missy Robbins' menus.

## Hero pattern
Photo carousel ("Slide 1 of 2") of dishes and dining space. Type minimal. Photography is high-craft, plated-pasta-portrait style — the food is treated like a subject, not a stock element.

## Navigation pattern
Fixed top nav, dual desktop/mobile structure. Menu items prioritize content over CTA: logo, main links, social. **Reservations is a secondary nav item — not a screaming button.** Also surfaces via footer modal form. Slightly less aggressive about pushing the booking than Carbone or Don Angie, which fits the "if you're here you already know" Brooklyn confidence.

## Menu page treatment
Rich HTML, single page, anchor-linked sections: *Antipasti, Pasta, Wine, Cocktails.* Wine subdivided by type with producer / vintage / region / grape. Items are simple text lists — no descriptions, no per-item photography, just dish name and price. There's a 5-slide image carousel within the menu page for context. Reads like a clean printed insert.

## Story / about page treatment
Carousel slider plus long-form narrative. **Founder-driven** — Missy Robbins front and center: Spiaggia, three NYT stars, Best Chef NYC 2018. Achievement-oriented and credential-forward, less family-narrative than Don Angie, less mythological than Carbone. Mood is professional-warm: pedigree + philosophy.

## One thing IT should adopt
**Strict monochrome system + one disciplined accent through photography only.** IT can stay almost entirely black, white, and warm paper — and let the food photography carry every drop of color. This is the easiest way to look premium on a real budget: instead of paying for a complex palette, pay for *one excellent photographer* and let the system be quiet around them.
