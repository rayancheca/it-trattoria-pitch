# Marea — Tear-down

**URL:** https://www.marea-nyc.com → redirects to https://marearestaurant.com
**Priority:** Secondary

---

## Type pairing
Clean, modern sans-serif system. "Marea" wordmark functions as the display moment; navigation and body share a contemporary sans. Less serif-traditional than the others in the set — Marea leans modern-fine-dining rather than old-world-trattoria.

## Color system
Minimalist white backgrounds with **yellow accent** (drawn from the brand octopus illustration) and neutral tones. The yellow is the outlier in the entire aspirational set — most peers refuse a chromatic accent. Marea uses it to claim a coastal / Mediterranean identity.

## Motion style
Video-driven hero with play/pause controls — more cinematic than the others. Suggests a "look at our beautiful room" pitch rather than a static editorial pitch.

## Hero pattern
Video loop in hero, with brand octopus illustration as a recurring mascot motif across the site. Coastal Italian identity reinforced visually rather than copy-driven.

## Navigation pattern
Clean horizontal nav anchored by **location switching** (NYC, Beverly Hills, Aspen). The brand operates as a small group, so location is the first hierarchy. Reservations integrated per-location.

## Menu page treatment
Standard upscale fine-dining menu treatment — clean HTML, dish names with brief Italian-rooted descriptions, no per-item photography. Wine and tasting menus split out.

## Story / about page treatment
Chef-led (Michael White lineage), Mediterranean philosophy, with the octopus / sea motif threaded throughout. More polished and group-restaurant feeling than the family-led shops like Don Angie.

## One thing IT should adopt
**A single signature illustration that earns its way onto everything.** Marea's octopus does more brand work than any photography or color choice on the site. IT could commission one small custom illustration — a sketched plate, a hand-drawn IT monogram, a pasta motif — and reuse it across the nav, the to-go bag, the menu header, and Instagram. Cheap, ownable, and immediately differentiating from generic counter-service templates.
