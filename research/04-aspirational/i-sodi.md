# I Sodi — Tear-down

**URL:** https://www.isodinyc.com
**Priority:** Secondary

---

## Type pairing
Serif headers (substantial weight, classic proportions) over a quiet sans-serif body. Italian-language accents used as typographic texture: "Tutti i giorni," "A tavola non si invecchia." Type does heritage signaling more than ornament — feels like a Tuscan farmhouse cookbook.

## Color system
Neutral, paper-and-cream foundation. Whites, soft grays, blacks. No accent chrome. Photography brings ochre, tomato, oil-and-rosemary tones. Almost no UI color — restraint is the brand.

## Motion style
Effectively none. Static photography, no carousels driving themselves, no scroll animation. The page feels like a print artifact.

## Hero pattern
Centered logo with atmospheric photography below. Large whitespace. Italian-language phrases as quiet ornamentation. Type-and-photo balance leans on whitespace as the third element.

## Navigation pattern
Straightforward horizontal nav, appears in two locations (top + secondary). Pragmatic items: Reservations, Delivery, Gift Cards, Press, Shop. No flashy CTA — Reservations is just an inline link, not a hero button.

## Menu page treatment
Minimal, restrained. Sections kept tight. Heavy use of whitespace. Italian section headers. No per-item photography.

## Story / about page treatment
Heritage narrative: Chef Rita Sodi's Tuscan farm origins, "time-honored methods." Generous text, readable, narrative-led. Builds authority through quietude.

## One thing IT should adopt
**Native Italian phrases used as design texture, not as translations.** "Tutti i giorni" doesn't need an English explanation — it sets mood. IT can place a couple of well-chosen Italian phrases as quiet typographic accents (section labels, footer signoff) without sliding into Olive-Garden Italian-American cliche.
