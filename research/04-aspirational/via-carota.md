# Via Carota — Tear-down

**URL:** https://www.viacarota.com
**Priority:** High

---

## Type pairing
Classic transitional serif for headings and the wordmark — elegant, restrained, slightly old-world (think Caslon / Sabon / Garamond family rather than a high-contrast Didone). Body and UI in a clean neutral sans-serif. Italian phrases ("Lunedì al Giovedi," "Venerdi, Sabato e Domenica") rendered in italics for cultural texture. Typography reads as understated, not theatrical — the opposite of Carbone's drama.

## Color system
Quiet and almost monastic:
- Warm white / paper background (`#FAF7F1`-ish)
- Near-black text (`#1A1A1A`-ish)
- No chrome accent color — photography supplies all warmth
- Imagery is muted, naturalistic, sometimes verging on B&W
Feels like a beautiful cookbook page, not a restaurant marketing site.

## Motion style
Minimal to none. Static photography, no carousels driving themselves, no scroll-triggered animation. The pace is the point — it forces you to slow down and read.

## Hero pattern
Centered serif type introducing the restaurant's philosophy, with a "Featured" image gallery below — food, interior details, dining moments. Type-and-photo balance is much closer to 50/50 than Carbone or Don Angie. The hero is *quiet*.

## Navigation pattern
Fixed horizontal top nav. Logo center/left. Menu items: Gatherings, Shop, Gift Cards, Cocktails, Takeout, Rita & Jody. **Reservations CTA appears mid-page as a visual banner with a Resy screenshot — not pinned in the top nav.** Unusual choice — confident, almost says "if you know, you know." Mobile collapses to hamburger.

## Menu page treatment
**Menu-as-image** — two large menu images (essentially scans of the printed menu) embedded on the page, plus a plain-text accessibility version. No fancy HTML grid, no per-item photography. The menu IS the artifact. This is the most "we made a beautiful printed menu and just took a picture of it" of any of the references — and it works.

## Story / about page treatment
Editorial, atmospheric, heritage-leaning. Copy about the 17th-century villa near Florence that inspired the name. Rita Sodi and Jody Williams positioned as the dual anchors. James Beard 2019 award noted but not shouted. Long text blocks with descriptive, slightly literary writing. Photography is curated objects, interiors, and food — not founder portraits front and center.

## One thing IT should adopt
**Menu-as-printed-artifact.** Instead of a clinical HTML menu, IT could photograph or scan a beautifully typeset printed menu and present it as the primary menu surface (with an accessible text version for screen readers). It immediately signals "this is a real restaurant with a real menu," not a Square / Toast template. Cheapest premium move on the entire list.
